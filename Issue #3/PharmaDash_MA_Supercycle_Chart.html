Bigger, not just busier

Average deal value jumped 44% in a single year — buyers are reaching for larger, nearer-to-market assets.

Reading the cycle

  • The trough was 2022. Post-pandemic, dealmaking dried up as balance sheets and valuations reset.
  • 2024 was a head-fake. Tariff worries, FDA leadership churn and MFN-pricing uncertainty kept deals small and value muted at ~$115B.
  • 2025 broke it open. A friendlier FTC, Fed rate cuts and a recovering biotech market pushed value to ~$209B.
  • 2026 is the breakout. $106B booked by early June puts the year on a ~$250B trajectory — with ~$1.3T in firepower still in reserve.
