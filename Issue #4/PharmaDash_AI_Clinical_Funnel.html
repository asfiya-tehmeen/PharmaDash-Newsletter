The core tension, in one chart

  • AI's superpower is early. An 80–90% Phase 1 success rate shows AI is excellent at designing molecules that are safe and drug-like — a real, measurable edge.
  • The edge vanishes in Phase 2. Once molecules must prove they actually work, AI drops to ~40% — the same bar everyone else clears. Designing a molecule isn't the same as curing a disease.
  • Phase 3 is the unanswered question. Too few AI drugs have reached late-stage trials to compare. The first wave of readouts lands over the next 18 months.
  • Zero approvals — so far. No fully AI-designed drug has been approved yet; the first is projected for 2026–27 at roughly 60% odds. The whole thesis rests on what comes next.
