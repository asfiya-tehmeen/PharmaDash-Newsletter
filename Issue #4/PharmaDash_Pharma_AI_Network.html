What the map shows

  • Two hubs anchor the network. Eli Lilly (3 AI partners) and Isomorphic Labs (3 pharma partners) are the most connected nodes — the gravity centers of the pharma–AI web.
  • Isomorphic is the connector. The Google DeepMind spinout links Lilly, J&J and Novartis, making one AI platform a shared dependency across rival drugmakers.
  • NVIDIA is the infrastructure layer. Its deals with Lilly and Amgen are about compute and platforms, not single molecules — the "picks and shovels" of the AI gold rush.
  • The big checks are rare and disclosed. Only two partnerships here carry a public $1B+ value; most deal terms stay private, so this map weights by count, not dollars.
