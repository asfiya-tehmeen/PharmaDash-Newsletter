Reading the takeoff

  • The curve is almost vertical. Value roughly doubled from 2023 to 2024, then nearly tripled into 2025 — the steepest climb of any sourcing trend in pharma right now.
  • Q1 2026 says it isn't slowing. A single quarter ($60B) already topped all of 2024, and China reportedly drove about two-thirds of global biotech deal value in that quarter.
  • It's volume and value together. Deal count nearly tripled in a year (65 → 186), so this isn't a few mega-deals skewing the total — it's a broad, structural shift.
  • Why: a $230B patent cliff meets cheaper science. Chinese assets carry upfronts 60–70% lower than Western ones, at increasingly comparable quality.
