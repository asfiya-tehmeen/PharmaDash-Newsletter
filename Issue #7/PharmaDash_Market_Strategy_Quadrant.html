Read the estimates honestly

OECD publishes per-capita pharma spend for developed economies. For India, Indonesia, Brazil, Saudi Arabia and the UAE it does not — so those points are PharmaDash estimates, derived as national market size ÷ population, and are drawn with dashed outlines. They are directional, not audited. Growth rates blend IQVIA (US, global) with commercial research firms (emerging markets), whose figures run higher and are not measured identically. Hover any bubble to see its inputs.
