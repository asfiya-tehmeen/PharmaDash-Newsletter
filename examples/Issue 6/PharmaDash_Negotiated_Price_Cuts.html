What the cuts reveal

  • The percentages are steep across the board — from 38% off Imbruvica to 79% off Januvia. Every one of the 10 was cut by at least a third.
  • The dollar stakes are enormous for a few. Eliquis alone drove $18.3B in annual Part D spend; a 56% cut on that base is where the real money moves.
  • Specialty biologics fall hardest in absolute terms. Stelara dropped $9,141 a month ($13,836 → $4,695), Imbruvica $5,615 — huge per-patient swings.
  • This is round one of many. Round 2 (15 drugs, incl. Ozempic/Wegovy) lands in 2027 at ~52% average; round 3 follows in 2028. The list compounds.
