Building America to beat the tariff

Facing a 100% tariff on imported drugs, pharma has pledged over $400 billion to build in the US. Here's where the biggest confirmed plants are landing — and which companies are spending the most.

Reading the buildout

  • North Carolina is the density hub. J&J, Amgen, Biogen, Roche and Novartis all have new projects there — on top of 100+ existing sites in the state.
  • Virginia and Texas host the biggest single plants. Lilly's $6.5B Houston site and $5B Richmond plant, plus Merck's $3B and AstraZeneca's $4.5B Virginia builds.
  • It's an API and biologics play. Almost every new plant targets active ingredients and biologics — the parts of the supply chain most exposed to the tariff.
  • Watch the gap between pledge and payback. These plants take years to build and ~8–10 years to pay off — so some of the $400B may be signaling as much as steel.
