The engines · Q1 2026 growth by product

Lilly's tirzepatide twins are doing the lifting — Mounjaro alone booked $8.7B and overtook Keytruda as the world's best-selling drug. Novo's obesity line still grows, but legacy diabetes is sliding.

Reading the divergence

  • Patents are destiny. Lilly says tirzepatide is protected into the back half of the 2030s; Novo faces semaglutide exclusivity loss in markets like China, Brazil and Canada — right now.
  • Price vs. volume. Novo's volumes are still climbing, but lower US realised prices are pulling revenue down faster than volume lifts it.
  • Even the "raise" is a smaller decline. Novo nudged guidance from −5%/−13% to −4%/−12%. Better — but still pointing down.
  • Lilly's floor is Novo's ceiling. The bottom of Lilly's guidance (~+26%) sits far above the best case in Novo's range (−4%).

The +32% mirage

+32%
reported
→
−4%
adjusted

Novo's headline Q1 sales jumped 32% at constant currency — but that was inflated by a one-time $4.2B (DKK 26.8B) reversal of 340B rebate provisions in the US. Strip the one-off out and underlying sales fell 4%. For 2026, always read Novo's adjusted line — the reported figure flatters a business that is contracting.
