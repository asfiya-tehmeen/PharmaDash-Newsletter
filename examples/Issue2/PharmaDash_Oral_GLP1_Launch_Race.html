Reading the curve

  • Brand recognition is the head start. The Wegovy pill carries the name of Novo's famous injectable, so it launched into demand that already existed. Foundayo is a brand-new name building awareness from zero.
  • Early Foundayo scripts were almost all cash-pay. Commercial coverage — including CVS Caremark, the largest U.S. pharmacy benefit manager — only came online in early June, a likely tailwind not yet in this curve.
  • Both pills are growing the market, not just splitting it. More than 80% of new Wegovy pill prescriptions come from patients new to GLP-1 therapy, and oral now makes up roughly 30% of the entire Wegovy brand.
  • Foundayo still beats the launches that came before it. Its trajectory runs ahead of where the injectable Wegovy and the Zepbound pen sat at the same stage — Novo's oral bar is simply exceptionally high.

Mind the undercount

IQVIA's weekly counts may miss prescriptions written through telehealth — and telehealth is roughly 35% of Foundayo's launch volume.

Lilly says about 20,000 patients are on Foundayo, versus the ~17,000 IQVIA logged at week 8. Read the red line as a conservative floor, not a ceiling.
