What the bubbles say

  • Oncology is the crowded lane. Three of these six deals target cancer — from Gilead's cell therapy to Lantheus's radiopharmaceuticals — mirroring oncology's ~39% share of all 2026 dealmaking.
  • The biggest check came from outside the West. India's Sun Pharma bought New Jersey's Organon for $11.75B, the year's largest deal so far.
  • Buyers are spreading their bets. Beyond oncology, the money is flowing into obesity (Merck/Terns), hematology (Incyte) and women's health — a broad-based cycle, not a single-therapy gold rush.
  • Modality diversity is the theme. In-vivo CAR-T, BCMA cell therapy, radiopharma and GLP-1 all feature — pharma is buying platforms as much as products.
