Why this is the engine of the M&A supercycle

  • 2028 is the danger year. Keytruda (~$29.5B), Eliquis (~$13B) and Opdivo (~$9B) all hit US exclusivity within months of each other — roughly $52B of revenue exposed at once.
  • This cliff is biologics, not pills. Unlike the 2010s Lipitor era, the biggest names here are complex biologics where erosion is slower but the dollars are far larger.
  • You can't grow a $10B drug in 18 months. So pharma buys one — which is exactly why dealmaking has surged in lockstep with the approaching cliff.
  • Merck and BMS are the motivated buyers. With ~47–56% of revenue exposed, they have the most pipeline holes to fill before 2028.
